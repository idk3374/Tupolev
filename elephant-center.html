Thai Elephant Conservation Center

History Information

The Thai Elephant Conservation Center (TECC), also known as the National Elephant Institute, was founded in 1993 and is located in Hang Chat District, Lampang Province.

The center focuses on elephant conservation, medical care, education, and sustainable tourism. It also works closely with Chiang Mai University’s Faculty of Veterinary Medicine and supports World Elephant Day.

🐘 Main Activities & Highlights

  • Elephant shows demonstrating intelligence and traditional skills
  • The world’s first elephant hospital
  • Mahout (elephant handler) training programs
  • Elephant bathing activities
  • Elephant feeding experiences
  • Elephant museum and cultural exhibits

Programs for Tourists

🎭 Short Program

Duration: 2–3 hours

  • ✓ Visit the elephant hospital and nursery
  • ✓ Learn basic elephant care
  • ✓ Elephant rides (15–60 minutes)
  • ✓ Daily elephant shows and bathing

Price: 200–300 Baht

🌿 Full Program

Duration: Half day (4–5 hours)

  • ✓ Educational visits
  • ✓ Feeding elephants
  • ✓ Mahout training experience
  • ✓ Lunch included

Price: 1,500–2,000 Baht

Visitor Information

📍 Location: Hang Chat District, Lampang Province

⏰ Opening Hours: Daily 08:00 – 17:00

💰 Entrance Fee: 50 Baht (activities not included)

🚗 Travel Time: About 45 minutes from Lampang city

📞 Booking: Advance booking is recommended

👕 What to Wear: Comfortable clothes and shoes

💡 Tips for Visiting

  • Visit early to avoid strong sunlight
  • Follow staff instructions
  • Bring a camera
  • Do not litter and respect animals
